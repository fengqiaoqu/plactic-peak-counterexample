## Verification and review status

The mathematical claim is the strict inequality $a_5=75095<88704=a_6$ in the specified $\binom{m}{k}$ basis. The package supplies a mathematical derivation, exact arithmetic certificates, and reproducible programs. Agreement between implementations checks the derivation and finite arithmetic; it is not an external referee report or a proof-assistant certification.

At preparation, the result had not received external peer review or endorsement from the cited authors. The search for earlier reports was targeted and cannot establish first discovery. The claim does not resolve the separate log-concavity clause or the general positive combinatorial-rule problem for Kronecker coefficients.
